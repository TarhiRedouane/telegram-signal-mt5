## Story
**As a** trader whose risk per trade is a fixed 3 % of the balance,
**I want** a setup leg to wait while the market is closer to its stop loss than the policy minimum (gold: 20 pips),
**so that** a leg filled next to its SL is never sized on a tiny distance and opened with a lot many times the normal size.

## What happens today (code trace at `30cd120`, reproduced 2026-10-15)

Every setup leg goes through `_fire_next_leg` (main.py:1239-1325) and is sized by `_order_volume` on `abs(fire price - SL)` (main.py:1423). Nothing sets a floor:

| Path | Fires when | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Complete multi-TP message (`SELL GOLD 4150 / SL 4160 / TP1 4140 / TP2 4130`) | `_validate_live_price` passes: market within `GOLD_MAX_PRICE_DEVIATION` (100) of the post's entry | main.py:1266-1271 |
| Zone, ladder, zone "buy now", confirmed rejection | price in the zone + tolerance, or `fire_now` | main.py:1272-1286 |
| "sell gold now" + entry (20.10) | inside the `entry_gap` band; the "better" side runs toward the SL, up to 10 points, stopped only at the SL itself (`fire_levels`) | main.py:1251-1265 |

Reproduced through `_on_telegram_message` + `_maybe_fire_setup` with the fakes of `test_single_signal_live_sizing.py` (balance $10,000, 3 %, tick 0.01 = $1/lot):
`SELL GOLD 4150 / SL 4160 / TP1 4140 / TP2 4130`, market 4159.8 → **two orders, SL 4160, 7.5 lots each**. The normal leg for a 10-point stop is 0.15.
Await-entry SELL, entry 4146, SL 4150: 0.37 lots at 4146, **15 lots at 4149.9**.

The sizing math holds only if the stop fills exactly at its price. The gold spread (0.2-0.4) can sit across a 0.2 stop. One point of slippage on 15 lots
costs $1,500 = 15 % of the balance, plus commission on 15 lots. The risk guard does not catch it: it uses the same distance (main.py:1396-1401).

## Story Context

**Existing System Integration:**
- `main.py` `_fire_next_leg` 1239-1325: builds the leg's signal per branch, then `_order_volume(signal, divisor)` (1294) and `_handle_parsed_signal` (1295).
  `_maybe_fire_setup` (1234-1237) loops while it returns True, so a `False` stops every leg due at that tick.
- `main.py` `_use_live_price_for_sizing` 1480-1536 (story 20.7): the single-signal minimum, `min(policy minimum, |post entry - post SL|)` (1515-1518), then moves the SL out.
- `src/signal_parser/symbol_policy.py` `get_min_sl_distance` (115-119): `GOLD_MIN_SL_DISTANCE`, default 2.0 price units (20 pips). Reuse it, no new setting.
- `PendingSetup` (setup_aggregator.py): `sl` is shared by every leg; `fire_levels` (480-493) gives the default SL (`GOLD_DEFAULT_SL_DISTANCE`, 10.0) when none was received;
  `low`/`high` are the entry zone (equal for a single entry; `None` while an await-entry setup has no entry, checked at main.py:1242).
- `_watch_setup` (1127-1175) polls every `setup_price_poll_seconds` (5 s) and drops a setup with no fill after `setup_idle_timeout_seconds` (300 s, from `last_update`).
- Technology: Python 3.12 asyncio, `Decimal` prices, pytest (`--strict-markers -W error`).

**Facts that shape the design:**
- **Wait, do not move the SL.** 20.7 moves a single signal's SL out to the minimum. A setup's SL is shared: the next `_apply_setup_levels` (996-1012, on any SL follow-up)
  pushes `setup.sl` to every leg and would pull a moved stop back in on the large lot. Not firing yet is the smallest safe change, and the price usually moves on.
- **Only legs with a received SL can be too close.** A leg without one gets the default SL at 10.0 from the fire price (`fire_levels`), above the 2.0 minimum.
- **One guard covers every branch.** Each branch uses `setup.sl` when it is set (`to_signal(sl=setup.sl)` in the complete branch, `fire_levels` returns `self.sl` otherwise).
  So the check can run once, near the top of `_fire_next_leg`, on `price` and `setup.sl`, before `_validate_live_price` (no "Live price check" log every 5 s while waiting).

## Rules (the behaviour to build)
1. Before a leg is built (any branch, leg 1 or later), when `setup.sl` is known, the market is still on the safe side of it (SELL: `price < setup.sl`;
   BUY: `price > setup.sl`) and `abs(price - setup.sl) < minimum`, the leg is not due: `_fire_next_leg` returns False. The setup stays armed; the next poll
   checks again. A price at or beyond the SL is not handled by this guard (rule 3).
2. `minimum` = `symbol_policy.get_min_sl_distance(setup.symbol)`, lowered to the author's own distance when that is tighter (decision **D1**):
   `min(policy minimum, distance from the SL to the nearest entry edge, low or high)`. No policy value (symbol without one) → no check, as today.
3. A price at or beyond the SL skips the guard and keeps today's behaviour (`fire_levels` → "invalidated", complete branch → MT5 refuses the stops).
4. One INFO log the first time a setup waits on this rule, not per poll: `Setup leg waiting chat=… SELL price 4159.8 is 0.2 from SL 4160 (minimum 2.0)`.
5. A setup that waits until the idle timeout is dropped as today ("expired before entry", or disarmed if it already owns legs). No trade.
6. Unchanged: complete single-TP signals (20.7 path), default-SL legs, leg count and ladder spacing, sizing for legs that do fire, risk guard, break-even.

## Decisions (D1 confirmed by user 2026-10-15)
- **D1 (confirmed by user 2026-10-15), author's tighter stop: yes.** Lower the minimum to the author's own distance from the nearest entry edge, as 20.7 does for single
  signals (consistent rule; a scalp author with a 10-pip stop still gets traded at their entry). Alternative: flat 2.0 minimum for setups; tight-stop setups then fire only
  when the price is 2.0 from the SL, often never, and expire.

## Technical Notes
- Why not reuse `_use_live_price_for_sizing`: it moves the SL and replaces the entry; setups must not move a shared SL (see Facts). Share only `get_min_sl_distance`.
- Mid price vs fill: the guard compares the mid price (`get_current_price`). A SELL fills at the bid, half a spread further from the SL, so the real distance is slightly larger.
- Out of scope: the TP-side deviation rule (`GOLD_MAX_PRICE_DEVIATION`, still a user decision from 20.7); F45 (direction check while a re-opened setup waits); partial fills (F41).

## Risk and Compatibility Check
- **Primary risk:** a setup that used to fire now waits and expires (missed trade when the market sits near the SL for 5 min). Accepted: that trade was the oversized one.
- **Secondary risk:** none from leg order. At one tick every leg sees the same `price` and the same `setup.sl`, so the guard lets all due legs fire or makes all of them
  wait; it never splits a ladder.
- **Rollback:** `git revert` the story commit. No DB, setting or prompt change.
