# Story 20.14: Opposite "Buy/Sell Gold Now" During an Open Trade Is a Retracement Trade Only When Far From Its Entry - Brownfield Addition

## Status
Draft

Source: user rule (2026-10-15), from the D5 discussion of story 20.13 / review finding F44 ([review-2026-10.md](../architecture/review-2026-10.md) section 7.2).
Replaces decision D5 of story 20.10 **for setups that own open legs**. Roadmap step 20, after 20.13.

## Story
**As a** trader holding an open trade when the author posts the opposite order,
**I want** the bot to refuse that order when the market is still near my trade's entry, and to take it as a small retracement trade when the market is far away,
**so that** my open trade keeps its break-even and invalidation management, and a real pull-back in a long move is still traded.

## The user's rule (2026-10-15)
- Opposite order **closer than 300 pips (30.0)** to the open trade's entry: it contradicts the trade. **Refuse it.** The open trade stays the chat's setup, fully managed.
- Opposite order **at least 300 pips** from the open trade's entry: the move has gone far and a retracement of 100-200 pips is expected before it continues. **Take it as a
  counter-trade**: one order, **half risk (1.5 %)**, **default TP 100 pips (10.0)** when the author gives none. The open trade keeps its break-even and invalidation management.

Example, SELL legs open at 4150 and 4155, SL 4160:

| Author posts | Distance from the nearest SELL entry (4150) | Bot |
|---|---|---|
| `buy gold now`, then `4132` | 18.0 (180 pips) | BUY refused. SELL setup unchanged. |
| `buy gold now`, then `4118` | 32.0 (320 pips) | BUY opened near 4118: SL 4108 (default), TP 4128 (default), 1.5 % risk. SELL legs keep break-even and invalidation. |

## What happens today (code at `30cd120`)
- Router (main.py:344-354): a price-less opener of the opposite side always calls `_start_awaiting_entry` (655-688), which `adopt`s a new setup in place of the old one.
  The old setup's watcher stops on its next loop (`while self._setup_aggregator.get(setup.chat_id) is setup`, main.py:1130). Its open legs keep their MT5 SL/TP but lose
  break-even at TP1 (`_track_setup_legs`), the candle-close invalidation (`_check_close_invalidation`) and SL/TP follow-ups. One WARNING lists the tickets.
- `SetupAggregator` (setup_aggregator.py:496-567) holds **one setup per chat** (`_setups: dict[int, PendingSetup]`); `drop` removes it only if it is still the chat's setup.
- Nothing checks the account's position mode. On a **netting** account a BUY next to a SELL does not open a second position: it reduces or closes the SELL. The only check in
  the code (`connection.py:703-704`, `validate_account`, dead code F6) reads `trade_mode` (demo/real), not `margin_mode`. `get_account_info()` (connection.py:624-665) already
  returns `margin_mode` (MT5: `ACCOUNT_MARGIN_MODE_RETAIL_HEDGING` = 2).

**Facts that shape the design:**
- **The open trade must keep being watched while another setup is the chat's setup.** Smallest change: the aggregator keeps a second list per chat, "held" setups
  (open legs, management only: break-even, invalidation, lifetime/idle drop; no new legs, no follow-ups). The watcher loop condition becomes "still the chat's setup or held".
- **Follow-ups go to the chat's setup, which is the counter-trade.** After the reversal, SL/TP messages apply to the counter-trade. The held trade keeps its MT5 SL/TP and
  its bot management, but no longer receives the author's level changes (log this once when holding).
- **Distance is measured twice with the same rule:** at the opener (live price, so a refused reversal never displaces the open trade) and when the counter leg fires
  (fire price, so an entry posted closer than 300 pips is refused too).

## Rules (the behaviour to build)
1. **Scope.** Applies when a price-less opener (20.13 short form) of the **opposite side** arrives and the chat's setup **owns at least one position**.
   A chat's setup with no position is replaced as today (D5 unchanged there). Same-side openers (D4) unchanged.
2. **Distance.** `distance` = smallest `abs(price - position.open_price)` over the setup's positions. `minimum` = `symbol_policy.get_min_reversal_distance(symbol)`
   (gold: new setting `GOLD_MIN_REVERSAL_DISTANCE`, default **30.0** = 300 pips). No policy value → refuse.
3. **At the opener**, with `price` = live price:
   - `distance < minimum` → refuse: nothing changes, one INFO `Reversal refused chat=… BUY: market 4132 is 18.0 from open SELL entry 4150 (minimum 30.0)`.
   - account is not hedging (`margin_mode` != retail hedging, or unreadable) → refuse, one WARNING naming the mode.
   - live price unavailable → refuse, one WARNING.
   - otherwise → the old setup becomes **held** (rule 5); a new await-entry setup of the opposite side becomes the chat's setup, marked **counter-trade**.
     One INFO `Reversal accepted chat=… BUY counter-trade (market 4118, 32.0 from open SELL 4150); SELL tickets [..] kept under management`.
4. **Counter-trade setup** (an await-entry setup, 20.10 rules for entry and band, plus):
   - one order only (`ladder=False`), whatever the number of TPs; TP = first TP received;
   - risk = half of `RISK_PERCENT` (constant in `main.py`, `COUNTER_TRADE_RISK_SHARE = 0.5`);
   - TP when the author gave none: fill price ± `symbol_policy.get_counter_tp_distance(symbol)` (gold: new setting `GOLD_COUNTER_TP_DISTANCE`, default **10.0** = 100 pips);
     an author TP received before or after the fill replaces it (after the fill through `_apply_setup_levels`, as today);
   - SL as today (author's, else the default 10.0; 20.12 minimum applies);
   - at fire time, the rule 2 distance is checked again with the fire price against the held setup's positions; under the minimum → not due (keep waiting, one INFO line).
5. **Held setup:** keeps break-even at TP1, candle-close invalidation, retries (20.2), lifetime and idle drop, exactly as when it was the chat's setup. It opens no leg
   (`armed = fire_now = False`) and receives no follow-up. When it ends (all legs closed, dropped), it leaves the held list. A held setup never becomes the chat's setup again.
6. Risk guard unchanged: it still counts the held legs (before break-even: 3 % open + 1.5 % counter = 4.5 %, under `MAX_OPEN_RISK_PERCENT` 6 %).

## Acceptance Criteria

**Functional Requirements:**
1. Market 4132, `buy gold now`: refused, INFO line; the chat's setup is the same SELL object; no new watcher. Then `4132`: no order.
2. Market 4118, `buy gold now`: SELL setup held, chat's setup = BUY counter-trade awaiting entry; INFO lines of rule 3. Then `4118`, price 4118.5: one BUY, SL 4108.5,
   TP 4128.5, volume = the 1.5 % lot for a 10.0 stop (0.15).
3. Counter-trade with `TP 4125` received before the entry: the BUY is sent with TP 4125. With `TP1 4125 / TP2 4135`: still one BUY, TP 4125.
4. Opener accepted at 4118, entry posted `4135`, price 4135: no order (17.0 from 4150), one INFO; the counter-trade expires after the idle timeout; the SELL is still held
   and managed.
5. Held SELL keeps break-even: after the reversal, leg 1 closes at TP ("tp"), leg 2 is moved to its entry (same as `test_break_even_retry.py` on a chat's setup).
6. Held SELL keeps the candle-close invalidation: an M`tf` close beyond the level closes its legs and drops it from the held list.
7. Netting account (`margin_mode` 0), market 4118: refused with WARNING; SELL setup unchanged.
8. Live price unavailable at the opener: refused with WARNING; SELL setup unchanged.
9. Chat's setup with **no** position (SELL waiting for its entry), `buy gold now` at any price: replaced by a BUY awaiting entry, as today (D5 unchanged without positions).
10. Same-side `sell gold now` after a fill (D4): unchanged.

## Decisions (confirmed by user 2026-10-15)
- **R1, threshold:** a reversal is accepted only at **300 pips (30.0) or more** from the open trade's nearest entry; closer → refused.
- **R2, counter-trade TP:** **default 100 pips (10.0)** from the fill when the author gives none; the author's TP replaces it.
- **R3, counter-trade risk:** **half risk** (1.5 % with `RISK_PERCENT` 3).
- **R4, netting account:** refuse (a BUY would net against the SELL). Architect's call from the code facts; tell the user at PO validation.

**Open (user, at PO validation):**
- **R5, priced reversals.** `BUY GOLD 4118 SL 4108 TP1 4128 TP2 4135` (a priced multi-TP or incomplete signal) still replaces the setup through `SetupAggregator.start`,
  and a complete single-TP opposite signal still opens on the instant path, both without the 300-pip rule. Recommended: same rule for priced reversals in a follow-up story
  (needs the instant path to look at the chat's setup); not in this story.

## Technical Notes
- Why "held" setups and not two setups per chat: everything except management (follow-ups, zone links, edits, re-arms) keeps reading one setup per chat; only the watcher
  loop and `drop` learn about the held list. Step 3 (persist state) must persist held setups too: note for that story.
- Hedging check at the opener only: the mode does not change while the terminal runs. Unreadable → refuse (fail closed, like the risk guard).
- Out of scope: R5; follow-ups for the held trade (step 13 edits); a held trade becoming the chat's setup again; partial close of the held trade at the reversal.

## Risk and Compatibility Check
- **Primary risk:** a real reversal under 300 pips is no longer traded (today it is, and the open trade loses its management). This is the user's rule.
- **Secondary risk:** two open directions on one symbol. Mitigation: hedging accounts only; half risk; risk guard counts both; default TP keeps the counter-trade short.
- **Third risk:** a held setup's watcher dies unnoticed. Mitigation: same watcher code and error path as today (`watcher error` drop with ERROR log); AC 5-6.
- **Rollback:** `git revert` the story commit. Two new settings with defaults; no DB, no prompt change.
